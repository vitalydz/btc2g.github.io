Bitcoin versus gold, or Bitcoin Gold (BTG)?

BTC2G compares Bitcoin (BTC) with gold. It does not forecast the price of Bitcoin Gold (BTG), a separate cryptocurrency. See the Bitcoin Gold project for that name.

How to read the relative-price scenario

The ratio divides a Bitcoin dollar reference price by the gold dollar reference price per troy ounce. If Bitcoin rises 10% while gold rises 5%, the ratio changes by 1.10 / 1.05 − 1, or about 4.76%. These are illustrative inputs, not a forecast.

Try your own inputs in the scenario calculator, or open the dashboard to check its published market date and source metadata. A model direction is not an executable quote or a guaranteed return.

What may influence the 2026 comparison

Several factors can affect Bitcoin and gold differently, including monetary policy, market liquidity, institutional demand, regulation, mining economics, and investor appetite for risk. Gold may benefit from defensive demand, while Bitcoin may respond more strongly to liquidity and adoption cycles.

Why use a BTC vs Gold model

Looking at Bitcoin only in dollars can miss relative performance against other scarce assets. A BTC vs Gold model helps frame whether Bitcoin appears strong or weak compared with a long-established store-of-value benchmark.

Risk-aware interpretation

Bitcoin can move sharply in both directions, and gold can also change with macro conditions. A forecast should be combined with risk management, independent research, and awareness that market conditions can change quickly.
