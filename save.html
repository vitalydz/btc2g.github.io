How the comparison works

Effective cost per BTC = total payment ÷ final BTC received. The difference is shown in satoshis (1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats). More BTC for the same payment means a lower effective cost in these entered quotes.

This does not isolate provider fees or spread, verify your quotes, or predict future savings. Bank charges outside a quote are not included. Compare the final provider checkout before purchasing.
