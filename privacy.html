Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. BTC2G offers optional user accounts and uses Cloudflare D1 to store account and aggregate traffic records. We do not collect or store payment card data. External providers may process data according to their own policies.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No payment card data stored by BTC2G
  • No custom geolocation tracking by BTC2G
  • No raw visitor IP addresses stored in the BTC2G analytics database

Third-Party Services

We use external providers like MoonPay, Topper, and Stripe for crypto purchase and subscription flows. Resend processes the email address and verification message needed to complete a new account registration, or when a signed-in user requests another verification message. These providers may process data according to their own policies.

Accounts

When you create an account, BTC2G stores your username, a one-way password hash with a unique salt, account timestamps, the email address supplied for verification, and your acceptance of the Terms and Privacy Policy. Passwords are not stored in plain text.

An email address is required for new ordinary accounts, and registration is counted as completed only after that address is confirmed. Before confirmation, BTC2G stores a pending technical account so you can sign in and request another message; it is excluded from completed-registration totals. A one-time verification token is stored as a one-way hash and expires after 30 minutes. Email verification identifies the address for account purposes; it does not by itself create a marketing subscription or activate Premium.

Administrators may protect sign-in with a passkey. BTC2G stores the passkey's public credential, usage counter, device category, backup status, and timestamps. The private key and biometric data remain with the administrator's device or passkey provider and are not sent to BTC2G.

Analytics

BTC2G counts aggregate page views and daily unique visitors using Cloudflare. A daily salted hash derived from IP address and browser user agent is used to prevent duplicate counts; the raw IP address and full user-agent string are not stored in the BTC2G database, and the daily hash is not intended to identify a person across days.

When a browser reports that a visit came from Product Hunt, BTC2G stores the source label producthunt, not the referring URL. A short-lived signed attribution token may be passed to the account page so a same-day registration can be counted as Product Hunt-attributed. The token does not contain the raw IP address or user-agent string.

Aggregate traffic records are automatically removed after 400 days.

Contact

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions, use BTC2G Support or email support@btc2g.com. We may request reasonable verification before acting on account data. Do not email passwords, passkeys, wallet secrets, payment-card numbers, or identity documents.
