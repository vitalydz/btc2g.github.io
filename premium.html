Built for transparency

Useful context without trading promises.

The product direction stays anchored to the same cautious principles as the public BTC2G model.

What the model is designed to show

  • Bitcoin’s relative valuation against gold using market-source price data.
  • How the latest ratio compares with a rolling historical window.
  • When the underlying data and model output were last updated.

What the model does not show

  • A guaranteed entry or exit point.
  • A short-term Bitcoin, gold, or portfolio return forecast.
  • Advice tailored to a reader’s finances, objectives, or risk tolerance.

Disclaimer: BTC2G Premium content and signals are model-based and provided for informational and educational purposes only. They are not financial advice, an offer to buy or sell an asset, or a guarantee of profit. Market data can be delayed or incomplete, assumptions can fail, and model signals can be wrong. Always conduct independent research and consider professional advice where appropriate.
